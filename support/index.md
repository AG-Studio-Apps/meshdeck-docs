## Contact us

Email **[{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }})**. Questions, bug reports, privacy requests and complaints all come here.

To help us help you, include:

- what you were doing, and what you expected;
- the message you saw, word for word if you can;
- your iOS version, and the version under **Settings → About** in meshDeck;
- how the host is reached: **SSH**, **Portainer** or **Advanced**, and whether through Tailscale.

<div class="note note--stop" markdown="1">
**Never send secrets**

Please do not email a password, an SSH key, an access token or an API key. We will never ask for one.
</div>

## Your subscription

meshDeck Pro is billed by Apple. To cancel or change it, open **Settings → your name → Subscriptions** on your iPhone, or use **Manage** on meshDeck's Pro screen. Refund requests go to Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). See [Free and Pro]({{ '/manual/pro/' | relative_url }}).

## Your data

Almost everything stays on your phone and your hosts. If you used Instant alerts and want your record removed from the relay, switch alerts off in the app, or email us. The [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) explains what the relay holds.

## Feature requests

We read them all. Tell us what you would like, and what you were trying to do when you wanted it.
