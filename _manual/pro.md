meshDeck is **free for one host**. meshDeck Pro adds every host you have and instant push alerts.

| | Free | meshDeck Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Hosts | One | Unlimited |
| Instant push alerts |, | Yes |
| Everything else | Yes | Yes |
| Account | None | None |

meshDeck Pro is an **annual, auto-renewing subscription** bought through the App Store. The price, and any free trial, are shown in the app before you subscribe.

## Subscribing

Open **Settings → meshDeck Pro** (it reads **Free · one host** or **Pro · unlimited hosts**) and tap **Upgrade**. On the paywall, tap **Subscribe**. Under the button you will see the trial or the price, for example "1 week free, then &lt;price&gt; / year". Nothing is charged until you confirm in the App Store's own sheet.

- **Restore purchases** brings back a subscription you already own, for example on a new phone.
- **Manage** opens the system subscription sheet, where you can cancel or change plan.
- **Terms** and **Privacy** open the [Licence Agreement]({{ '/eula/' | relative_url }}) and [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

A subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the period. You manage it in your Apple Account settings.

## If it lapses

Your hosts are kept, and so is everything you set up. Hosts beyond the first are not run, and show a lock. Opening one shows "&lt;name&gt; needs meshDeck Pro. Your subscription lapsed. The host is saved; renew to reconnect it." with a **Renew** button. Renewing brings them straight back.

The first host you added stays fully working, on the free plan.
